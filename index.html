Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. The floating window, cinema mode and all playback controls are free forever. PRO adds the engagement score, movie finder and sponsored-reel skipping.

How do I activate PRO?

After checkout you get a license key by email. Open the PiP Booster popup in Chrome, paste the key and click Activate.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

One license works on up to 3 browsers at a time. Deactivate one from the popup to move it.

Does it work on Mac?

Yes — Chrome on Windows, macOS and Linux (Chrome 116 or newer).

Do you collect my Facebook data?

No. Reels, comments and stats are processed locally and never leave your browser. Read the privacy policy.
