[ faq ]

Questions, answered

Which sites does it work on?

Facebook Reels today. YouTube (videos and Shorts) and Netflix arrive with version 1.1, which is in review on the Chrome Web Store. Once it's live, your installed extension updates automatically.

How do I keep subtitles in picture in picture?

Turn on subtitles on the site as usual (the CC button on YouTube, the subtitle menu on Netflix), then click the PiP button. PiP Booster copies each line into the floating window as it appears.

Does it work with Netflix?

Yes. The video plays in the floating window at the same quality as in the tab. Picking subtitle and audio language, skipping the intro and going to the next episode are free; showing Netflix subtitles inside the floating window and skipping intros automatically are PRO. Nothing is recorded or downloaded.

Is it really free?

Yes. The floating window, cinema mode, all playback controls, the viral score and everything the site already offers (on YouTube: captions, chapters, quality, loop; on Netflix: subtitle and audio language, skip intro) are free forever. PRO adds what we built on top: Netflix subtitles in the window, automatic intro skipping, skipping YouTube sponsor segments, A-B loop, speed remembered per channel, window size memory, auto-play next, skipping boring and sponsored reels, next-part links and the movie finder.

Can I try PRO first?

Yes. Every new install gets all PRO features free for 24 hours. No sign-up, no card. After that, the free features keep working.

How do I activate PRO?

After checkout you get a license key by email. Open the PiP Booster popup in Chrome, paste the key and click Activate. One license works on up to 3 browsers.

Does it work on Mac?

Yes. Chrome 116 or newer on Windows, macOS and Linux.

Do you collect my data?

No. Videos, subtitles and page content are processed locally and never leave your browser. The only lookup is the optional sponsor-segment check on YouTube (PRO), which sends a 4-character hash, not the video ID. Read the privacy policy.

What if it doesn't work for me?

Request a refund within 14 days and you get your money back in full. See the refund policy.
