1. The product

PiP Booster ("the extension") is a browser extension for Google Chrome that lets you watch videos from supported sites (YouTube, Netflix and Facebook Reels) in a Picture-in-Picture window or a cinema view. The free features are available to everyone. PRO features require a paid license.
