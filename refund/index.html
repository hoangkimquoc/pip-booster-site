Refund Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

We want you to be happy with PiP Booster PRO. Try the free features first — they work without a license.

14-day money-back guarantee

If PRO does not work for you, request a refund within 14 days of purchase and we will refund you in full. No questions asked.

How to request a refund

  • Customer portal: open the order link in your Polar receipt email to view your purchase and contact us about it.
  • Support form: open a support request with the email you used at checkout (do not post your license key).

How refunds work

  • Refunds are issued through Polar, our merchant of record, to the original payment method. It may take 5–10 business days to appear on your statement.
  • When a purchase is refunded, its license key is revoked and PRO features are disabled.
  • After 14 days, refunds are considered case by case — for example, if a PRO feature stops working and we cannot fix it.
